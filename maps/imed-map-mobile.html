Map key

This map shows the most environmentally deprived neighbourhoods on a scale of 1 to 10, where 10 is the most deprived and 1 is the least.

The IMED combines data on pollution, nature deficit and climate extremes, and reflects where people live and their proximity to environmental hazards in each neighbourhood.
